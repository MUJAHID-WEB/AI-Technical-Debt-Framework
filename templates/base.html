The Core Problem

AI components introduce a new, "sticky" kind of technical debt. Unlike traditional code debt, AI debt includes hidden feedback loops, explosive glue code, and undeclared consumer dependencies.

Our research defines the "Model Entanglement Score" to quantify how deeply AI models are coupled with your infrastructure, impacting overall system maintainability.
